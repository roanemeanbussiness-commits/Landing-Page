03 · Process

From audit to live, in four weeks.

A defined engagement with a defined end: a running system, a documented operating manual, and full handover. Your team's total time commitment is five to eight hours.

Week 1

The audit

A recorded working session through real scenarios under real pressure: pricing conflicts, difficult clients, hiring calls. Plus your services, processes, and twenty to thirty real messages, verbatim. It produces your Company Operating Manual.

Weeks 1 and 2

The operator

Your named operator is configured and deployed on your own infrastructure. The first system is scoped against the sharpest pain the audit found, usually reception or lead qualification.

Weeks 2 and 3

First system live

The first function goes into production and starts producing its named outcome. The second is scoped while the first proves itself on live traffic.

Weeks 3 and 4

Handover

The second system goes live, memory is seeded with your business, and everything is handed over: source code in your repo, credentials under your control, the manual documented.

Month 2 onward

Expansion

On retainer, the system earns its own growth. Each new department is added once the last one has proved out. Structural growth, not a subscription.
